# Report template

Structure for a single-ticker report. Adapt headings to the ticker; keep the
order — verdict first, evidence after, conditions last.

---

# {COMPANY} ({TICKER}) — Risk {SCORE}/10, {LEVEL}

**As of {DATE} · ${PRICE} · {CHANGE}% on the day**

## The read

Two to four sentences. The risk score, what percentile that is for this ticker,
and the one sentence that reconciles the technical read with the fundamental
one. If the two disagree, say which you weight and why.

## Where price sits

| Level | Value | Price vs. level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8-week EMA | ${} | {}% |
| 21-week EMA | ${} | {}% |
| 50-week SMA | ${} | {}% |
| 200-week SMA | ${} | {}% |
| 52-week high | ${} | {}% |
| All-time high | ${} | {}% |

One paragraph on what that structure means: trending or reverting, which average
is acting as support, what price would move it into the next band down.

## Historical base rate

Of the {N} sessions {TICKER} has spent in the {BAND} risk band, the median
forward return was {}% at 3 months and {}% at 12 months, with a {}% win rate at
12 months — and a worst case of {}%.

Then one sentence on how much to trust it: sample size, overlap, and whether
the regime those samples came from resembles today's.

## The business

What it sells, who pays, and the two or three things that actually drive the
numbers. Short — this is context, not a company profile.

## Latest results

Most recent quarter with the date: revenue and growth, margins, EPS, guidance,
and how it landed versus expectations. Note the trend across the last few
quarters if it's the story.

## Valuation

Current and forward P/E, P/S or EV/EBITDA as appropriate, each against the
ticker's own 5-year range and its closest peers. State plainly whether the
multiple is expanding or compressing and what's driving it.

## Catalysts

- Next earnings: {DATE}
- {Upcoming events, product cycles, decisions with dates where possible}

## Risks

Three to five specific, falsifiable risks. Not "competition" — name the
competitor and the exposed revenue line.

## What would change this read

- **More constructive if:** {condition, with a number or a price}
- **More cautious if:** {condition, with a number or a price}

---

*Analysis as of {DATE}. Risk score is a technical measure of price extension
versus moving-average structure; it is not a valuation model. Historical base
rates describe past behaviour and do not predict future returns. This is
research and information, not investment advice.*
